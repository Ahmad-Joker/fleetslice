# Measured experiments

Run date: 10 November 2026. Seed 42; 2,000 bootstrap resamples; minimum slice size 10.

## Controlled regression fixture

200 synthetic scenes. Both forecast files are generated by adding deliberate errors to known future positions. This tests the evaluator, not predictor quality. All three global metrics are within tolerance; the turning slice regresses.

| Scope | Metric | Baseline | Candidate | Delta | 95% interval | Decision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| all (n=200) | ade_m | 0.8985 | 0.3897 | -0.5088 | [-0.5707, -0.4405] | within_tolerance |
| all (n=200) | fde_m | 0.8985 | 0.3897 | -0.5088 | [-0.5703, -0.4430] | within_tolerance |
| all (n=200) | miss_rate | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | +0.0000 | [0.0000, 0.0000] | within_tolerance |
| behavior:turning (n=20) | ade_m | 0.9016 | 1.7999 | +0.8983 | [0.8879, 0.9091] | regression |
| behavior:turning (n=20) | fde_m | 0.9016 | 1.7999 | +0.8983 | [0.8885, 0.9088] | regression |
| behavior:turning (n=20) | miss_rate | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | +0.0000 | [0.0000, 0.0000] | within_tolerance |

Overall gate: **regression**. This is the intended result despite the improved global displacement averages.

## Official AV2 validation sample

Downloaded 128 parquet scenes (18,720,080 bytes) from the official public Argoverse S3 validation prefix. Selection: first 128 scenario parquet keys in lexicographic order. Converted all 128 focal tracks; zero skips. Each uses 50 observed and 60 future positions at 10 Hz. No training or tuning was performed.

Baseline: constant velocity. Candidate: acceleration-capped constant acceleration. The candidate uses only history. It is a deliberately simple alternative reference, with no expectation that adding acceleration will improve noisy six-second extrapolations.

| Metric | Constant velocity | Constant acceleration | Delta | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| ade_m | 4.3541 | 7.9583 | +3.6042 | [2.1654, 5.0785] |
| fde_m | 11.3920 | 23.8980 | +12.5060 | [8.4506, 16.9530] |
| miss_rate | 0.8672 | 0.9688 | +0.1016 | [0.0469, 0.1641] |

Overall gate: **regression**. CA performed worse on this sample. No accuracy gain is claimed. FDE is large for both references; the sample is difficult and the models do not use maps or interactions.

ADE and FDE are metres. Miss rate is a fraction, using endpoint error strictly greater than 2 m. For example 0.8672 is approximately 86.72%, not 0.8672%.

This sample is small, nonrandom, and includes sparsely populated slices. It cannot establish representative fleet performance, statistical independence of all source clips, Tesla performance, or an official AV2 benchmark score. Intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiplicity.
